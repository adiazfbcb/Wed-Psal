Dear Church Family,

There is never a moment of our lives where worship is not happening. Whatever is at the center of the heart, mind, and habits—that is what we worship. As we abide in the Living Word, our mind’s attention and heart’s affection will be directed more and more towards Christ, making Him the worthy center of our worship. As the songbook of the Bible, the Psalms teach us how to worship in every season of life.

May we become a people who instinctually turn to the Word of God, seeking His face and voice. We pray that the Wednesday Psalter will be a means of returning to the simple practice of opening God’s Word and finding truth for our day.

God is still revealing Himself and His people are still responding. May we enter our gathering on Sunday already worshipping.

READ

Psalm 8 (ESV)

How Majestic Is Your Name

[1] O Lord, our Lord,
   how majestic is your name in all the earth!
You have set your glory above the heavens.

[2]    Out of the mouth of babies and infants,
you have established strength because of your foes,
   to still the enemy and the avenger.

[3] When I look at your heavens, the work of your fingers,
   the moon and the stars, which you have set in place,

[4] what is man that you are mindful of him,
   and the son of man that you care for him?

[5] Yet you have made him a little lower than the heavenly beings
   and crowned him with glory and honor.

[6] You have given him dominion over the works of your hands;
   you have put all things under his feet,

[7] all sheep and oxen,
   and also the beasts of the field,

[8] the birds of the heavens, and the fish of the sea,
   whatever passes along the paths of the seas.

[9] O Lord, our Lord,
   how majestic is your name in all the earth!

REFLECT

Reflection

Psalm 8 reminds us of our own smallness and the Lord’s incomprehensible kindness. Just by gazing at a small part of His heavenly creation, we are reminded of our own insignificant frame. However, the Lord has formed us in His image, crowned us with glory and honor, and given us dominion over the wonders of His creation. Though we are but dust, the Maker of the Universe has made us a little lower than the angels, displaying not only His power, but His grace and care to us as the work of His hands. May we stop amid the craziness of life to harmonize with the song of creation that proclaims the power and kindness of our Creator God.

PRAY

Your time to respond

Thank the Lord for His majesty, noting specific aspects of creation that remind you of His power.

Thank Him for being mindful of your frame and consistently caring for you.

Ask Him to open your eyes to the wonders of His creation and its testimony to His character.

Ask the Lord to strengthen your childlike faith in His power to still the specific enemies and avengers in your life or the lives of those around you.

Dedicate this week to the Lord and commit to coming into our gathering on Sunday already worshipping.
