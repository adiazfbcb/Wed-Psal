God is still revealing Himself

Dear Church Family,

There is never a moment of our lives where worship is not happening. Whatever is at the center of the heart, mind, and habits—that is what we worship. As we abide in the Living Word, our mind’s attention and heart’s affection will be directed more and more towards Christ, making Him the worthy center of our worship. As the songbook of the Bible, the Psalms teach us how to worship in every season of life.

May we become a people who instinctually turn to the Word of God, seeking His face and voice. We pray that the Wednesday Psalter will be a means of returning to the simple practice of opening God’s Word and finding truth for our day.

God is still revealing Himself and His people are still responding. May we enter our gathering on Sunday already worshipping.

Psalm 25 (ESV)

Teach Me Your Paths

[1] To you, O LORD, I lift up my soul.

[2] O my God, in you I trust;
let me not be put to shame;
let not my enemies exult over me.

[3] Indeed, none who wait for you shall be put to shame;
they shall be ashamed who are wantonly treacherous.

[4] Make me to know your ways, O LORD;
teach me your paths.

[5] Lead me in your truth and teach me,
for you are the God of my salvation;
for you I wait all the day long.

[6] Remember your mercy, O LORD, and your steadfast love,
for they have been from of old.

[7] Remember not the sins of my youth or my transgressions;
according to your steadfast love remember me,
for the sake of your goodness, O LORD!

[8] Good and upright is the LORD;
therefore he instructs sinners in the way.

[9] He leads the humble in what is right,
and teaches the humble his way.

[10] All the paths of the LORD are steadfast love and faithfulness,
for those who keep his covenant and his testimonies.

[11] For your name’s sake, O LORD,
pardon my guilt, for it is great.

[12] Who is the man who fears the LORD?
Him will he instruct in the way that he should choose.

[13] His soul shall abide in well-being,
and his offspring shall inherit the land.

[14] The friendship of the LORD is for those who fear him,
and he makes known to them his covenant.

[15] My eyes are ever toward the LORD,
for he will pluck my feet out of the net.

[16] Turn to me and be gracious to me,
for I am lonely and afflicted.

[17] The troubles of my heart are enlarged;
bring me out of my distresses.

[18] Consider my affliction and my trouble,
and forgive all my sins.

[19] Consider how many are my foes,
and with what violent hatred they hate me.

[20] Oh, guard my soul, and deliver me!
Let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in you.

[21] May integrity and uprightness preserve me,
for I wait for you.

[22] Redeem Israel, O God,
out of all his troubles.

Reflection

We have been given the gift not only of salvation, but also of friendship with the Lord and assurance that He will never put us to shame. May we desire to follow in His ways, and yield our own will so that we can become more like Christ each day. May we be a people of confession, coming to the Lord daily for grace and a renewal of right desires. All of our troubles, habits, and struggles are seen by the Lord, and He is faithful to forgive. Let us take refuge in His redemption.

Your time to respond

Thank the Lord for being trustworthy when He says that your sins are forgiven; that you no longer are bound by your old ways.

Thank Him for His mercy and love that have been to all generations - take a moment to consider the amazing reality that the God who kept His promise to Abraham is the same God Who hears your prayers.

Ask Him to take away your sinful habits so that you can walk in the way He is directing you.

Ask the Lord to see the things that are troubling you and causing you pain, and ask Him for the strength to trust Him.

Dedicate this week to the Lord and commit to coming into our gathering on Sunday morning already worshipping.
