THE WEDNESDAY
PSALTER

God is still revealing Himself
and His people are still responding.

Dear Church Family,

There is never a moment of our lives where worship is not happening. Whatever is at the center of the heart, mind, and habits—that is what we worship. As we abide in the Living Word, our mind’s attention and heart’s affection will be directed more and more towards Christ, making Him the worthy center of our worship. As the songbook of the Bible, the Psalms teach us how to worship in every season of life.

May we become a people who instinctually turn to the Word of God, seeking His face and voice. We pray that the Wednesday Psalter will be a means of returning to the simple practice of opening God’s Word and finding truth for our day.

God is still revealing Himself and His people are still responding. May we enter our gathering on Sunday already worshipping.

Psalm 146

Put Not Your Trust in Princes

[1] Praise the LORD!
Praise the LORD, O my soul!

[2] I will praise the LORD as long as I live;
I will sing praises to my God while I have my being.

[3] Put not your trust in princes,
in a son of man, in whom there is no salvation.

[4] When his breath departs, he returns to the earth;
on that very day his plans perish.

[5] Blessed is he whose help is the God of Jacob,
whose hope is in the LORD his God,

[6] who made heaven and earth,
the sea, and all that is in them,
who keeps faith forever;

[7] who executes justice for the oppressed,
who gives food to the hungry.
The LORD sets the prisoners free;

[8] the LORD opens the eyes of the blind.
The LORD lifts up those who are bowed down;
the LORD loves the righteous.

[9] The LORD watches over the sojourners;
he upholds the widow and the fatherless,
but the way of the wicked he brings to ruin.

[10] The LORD will reign forever,
your God, O Zion, to all generations.
Praise the LORD!

Reflection

The psalmist seems to be unable to contain his praise, bursting forth with a call to praise the Lord, regardless of earthly circumstances. In light of all that God has done, we are admonished to remember the miraculous ways in which God has moved and to respond with a song of thanksgiving and trust. In all areas of life where injustice, pain, and suffering exist - the captive, the forgotten, the homeless, the lonely and the fatherless - the Lord is there, and the Lord is moving. We can rest in that knowledge that all wrongs will be righted, and we are held by the God of all generations.

Your time to respond

Thank the Lord for the ability to praise Him and for giving you a mind capable of recognizing His work in the world.

Thank Him for being sovereign - that you do not have to find confidence in the fleeting power of man or in the strength of your body. Thank God for being your help and Creator.

Ask Him to execute justice for the oppressed and to feed those hungering for renewal.

Ask the Lord to give you a love for righteousness and to give you His heart for the people around you, your city, and the nations.

Dedicate this week to the Lord, and commit to coming into our gathering on Sunday already worshipping.
